- Bree cron: `"4 * * * *"` (2 minutes after `load` at :02).
- Read from ClickHouse table `Assets`.
- Normalize ALL numeric columns (`open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`) — NOT `asset_type` nor `timestamp`.
- Apply mathematically sound normalization to [0, 1] per feature (min-max over historical range or rolling/global stats — choose approach that preserves ML context; document briefly in code).
- Persist to new ClickHouse table `assets_parsed` (create DDL + helpers in `utils/clickhouse.ts`, same engine/partition style as Assets).
- Idempotent: every `(asset_type, timestamp)` in Assets must exist in `assets_parsed`; skip rows already parsed — no duplicates.
- Register job in `cron/src/index.ts`.
